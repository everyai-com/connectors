# LeaseBreak quick start

LeaseBreak turns an early move-out into numbers and paperwork. It uses only the terms
the user provides and never gives legal advice.

Suggested flow:

1. Collect the basics: monthly rent, months left, deposit, the lease's break fee and
   notice period, and any fixed re-letting fee.
2. `estimate_break_cost` for the itemised cost. If the unit re-lets fast, pass
   `expected_relet_months` to reflect the reduced rent-until-relet.
3. `compare_options` to weigh break vs sublet vs staying; present the cheapest path
   and its risks.
4. `negotiation_checklist` before they talk to the landlord.
5. `notice_letter` when they are ready to send formal notice; remind them to send by
   email AND tracked mail, and not to stop paying rent until a signed agreement exists.
6. Always relay the disclaimer: informational self-help, not legal advice.
