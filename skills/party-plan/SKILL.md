# PartyPlan quick start

PartyPlan is the maths and drafting half of party planning: it allocates the
budget, sizes the shopping from the invite list, lays out a countdown timeline
and builds the checklist. It makes no bookings and moves no money.

Suggested flow:

1. Get the budget, guest count and party date up front.
2. `party_budget` splits spend across venue, food and drink, cake, decorations,
   entertainment, favors and contingency - pick the budget, standard or
   premium style to shift the percentages.
3. `headcount_plan` converts invited guests (and an expected decline) into
   expected attendance, adults/children split and shopping quantities.
4. `party_timeline` works back from the event date with dated tasks from
   T-42 days to the day itself.
5. `party_checklist` covers what to organise for a home or venue party; add
   extras keywords like "pool", "bbq", "costume" or "outdoor" for specific
   items and safety notes.
